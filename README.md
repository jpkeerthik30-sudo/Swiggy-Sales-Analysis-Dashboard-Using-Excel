 🍽️ Swiggy Sales Analysis Dashboard Using Microsoft Excel
📌 Project Description

This project is an interactive Swiggy Sales Analysis Dashboard created entirely in Microsoft Excel to analyze food delivery performance across various cities and states in India. The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights through dynamic charts, KPI cards, slicers, and data visualizations.

The dashboard enables users to monitor sales trends, customer ratings, order volumes, food category preferences, and regional performance. It helps stakeholders identify growth opportunities, understand customer behavior, and make data-driven business decisions.

🎯 Project Objectives
Analyze overall sales and order performance.
Track customer satisfaction through ratings.
Compare sales across different food categories.
Identify top-performing cities and states.
Monitor monthly, weekly, and daily sales trends.
Provide actionable insights for business growth.

📈 Key Insights
Non-Veg food contributes approximately 63% of total sales, making it the dominant category.
Bengaluru is the highest revenue-generating city with over ₹5.4M in sales.
Weekend sales outperform weekday sales, with Saturday recording the highest revenue.
Customer satisfaction remains strong with an average rating of 4.34.
Sales performance is relatively stable across weeks, indicating consistent customer demand.
